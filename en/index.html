Responsible Operations

Less Waste. Better Planning.

We plan quantities, service formats and logistics to reduce food waste and unnecessary materials without compromising service.

  • Compostable or biodegradable materials when practical for the service.
  • Portion planning and replenishment to help reduce food waste.
  • Fewer single-use plastics when suitable alternatives are available.
  • Coordinated transport routes and logistics for every project.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Production Teams Usually Need to Know.

Clear answers to help you plan and request a quote.

Do you cater film sets and production shoots?

Yes. We plan meals for crew, talent and clients around call times, locations, meal breaks and the production schedule.

Can you cover breakfast, lunch and dinner in one day?

Yes. We can coordinate multiple meals, plus snacks, hydration and scheduled or continuous coffee service.

Do you offer boxed meals for crew and staff?

Yes. Individual meals work well for staggered calls, travel and short meal windows.

Can you keep a coffee station running all day?

Yes. Our coffee service can include coffee, tea, milk options, water, fruit, pastries and snacks, depending on scope.

Can you cater locations without a kitchen?

Yes. We assess access, power, water and available space before recommending the best setup.

Do you cater corporate events too?

Yes. We cater meetings, conferences, conventions, activations and executive events with flexible service formats.

Do you work outside Mexico City?

Yes. We coordinate service across Mexico according to dates, volume, logistics and project scope.

Can you work within our budget?

Yes. Headcount, meal frequency, menu, presentation, venue and service format all help shape the proposal.

What details do you need for a quote?

Dates, city or venue, approximate schedule, headcount and meal times. Guest profiles and format preferences also help.
